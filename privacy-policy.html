Yofit’s AI & privacy promise

Our AI models are independently developed, trained, deployed, and operated by Yofit. We do not use third-party AI model services. We do not collect information unrelated to Yofit’s functions. We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. We apply strict safeguards to protect your privacy.

Privacy in plain language

This Policy explains information Yofit processes, why we need it, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. We collect only information connected to the features you use.

No side business built on your data.
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information, including for targeted advertising or data-broker activity.
2

Information you provide

Account and profile

We may process an account identifier, display name, profile photo, bio, and contact details you choose to provide.

Creative inputs and outputs

When you use Artistic Fusion or related tools, we process the portrait, art reference, style choice, prompt, and resulting image needed to complete your request.

Community and support

Posts, captions, likes, messages, reports, and support correspondence are processed so those features work. Please avoid sending sensitive information you do not need to share.

3

Information created by use

We may process limited technical information such as app version, device type, language, coarse region, crash diagnostics, feature events, security logs, and timestamps. We use it to operate, protect, and improve Yofit.

We do not collect precise location, contacts, health data, unrelated browsing history, or unrelated device content unless a future feature clearly requires it and first asks for permission.

4

Photos and camera access

Photo-library or camera access occurs only after you choose the feature and grant system permission. You can change permission in device settings.

Selected images are processed to provide the requested creation, upload, profile, or message feature. We do not scan your full photo library. Image metadata that is not needed may be removed or ignored.

5

How our AI handles content

Our AI models are self-developed, self-trained, self-deployed, and operated by Yofit. We have not used third-party AI model services for Yofit’s generation features, and we do not transmit your creative inputs to outside AI model providers.

Uploaded images may pass through our controlled processing systems to generate the requested result and detect prohibited or harmful use. We do not use private photos or private messages to train our AI models unless we first obtain a separate, explicit, informed opt-in.

6

Why we process information

  • Provide accounts, discovery, creation, posting, messaging, and support.
  • Personalize choices you make, such as saved styles and language.
  • Protect users, investigate reports, prevent abuse, and secure access.
  • Fix crashes, measure reliability, and improve feature performance.
  • Comply with valid legal obligations and enforce our agreements.

Depending on where you live, our legal bases may include performing our contract with you, consent, legitimate interests in safe operation, and compliance with law.

7

No sale, rental, trade, or sharing

Yofit does not sell, trade, rent, or share user information. We do not disclose user information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Information may be disclosed only in narrowly necessary circumstances: when you intentionally publish or send it to another user; when required by a valid legal process; during an organizational transaction subject to confidentiality and continued privacy protections; or to prevent imminent harm.

Technical infrastructure vendors, if used for basic hosting, delivery, or support operations, may process limited information solely under Yofit’s instructions and confidentiality safeguards. They are not permitted to use it for their own purposes, and they are not AI model service providers.

8

Retention and deletion timeline

While your account is active

We retain account and user-selected content as needed to provide the Services.

After you delete

We delete or de-identify information within a reasonable operational period, generally within 30 days, unless a longer period is required for security, dispute resolution, payment records, or law.

Backups

Residual encrypted backup copies may persist for a limited rotation period and are isolated from ordinary use.

9

How we protect information

We use access controls, encryption in transit, protected storage, environment separation, logging, and staff confidentiality practices appropriate to the sensitivity of information.

No system can guarantee absolute security. If an incident creates a legally reportable risk, we will investigate and notify affected users and authorities as required.

10

Your controls and rights

You can edit profile information, delete posts, manage photo and notification permissions, leave conversations, and request account deletion. Depending on your location, you may also request access, correction, deletion, portability, restriction, or objection.

Email support@yofit.help with “Privacy Request” and the relevant account details. We may verify identity before fulfilling a request. Authorized agents may submit requests where permitted by law.

11

Children and international use

Yofit is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child provided information, contact us so we can investigate and delete it.

Yofit may be accessed across borders. Where information is processed outside your country, we apply protections required by applicable law and this Policy.

12

Policy updates and contact

We may update this Policy as Yofit evolves. We will post the new effective date and provide additional notice when a change materially affects your rights or how information is used.

Privacy questions and requests can be sent to support@yofit.help.

Quick answers

Frequently asked

Does Yofit use outside AI model providers?

No. Yofit’s AI models are developed, trained, deployed, hosted, and operated by us.

Does Yofit sell or share my data?

No. We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user information.

Does Yofit scan my whole photo library?

No. System permission lets you select content; we process the items you choose for a feature.

Yes. Email support@yofit.help or use an available in-app deletion control. Limited records may be retained where required for security or law.
